What sets it apart

Everything in AirPods 5, plus these extras:

01

Wireless charging

Charges via USB-C, Apple Watch charger, or a Qi pad.

Drop the case on any Qi-certified charger, or pop it on an Apple Watch charger — no cable hunting. Perfect for desks, nightstands, and car mounts.

02

Volume swipe

Swipe up or down on the AirPod stem to adjust volume.

Slide your thumb along the stem to raise or lower the volume without reaching for your phone — a quick gesture that stays out of your way.

03

Find My

A built-in speaker on the case for precise location tracking.

Misplaced the case? Ring it from the Find My app — the built-in speaker helps you track it down to the inch, even tangled under the couch.

04

More battery

Up to 5 hours of listening (vs 4) on a single charge.

A bigger case battery adds about an hour per charge — up to 22 hours total with the case — so it lasts a full week on your commute.
