Highlights

Get the highlights.

01

Active Noise Cancellation

Up to 1.5x more noise blocked than the previous generation.

The H2 chip analyzes and cancels background noise in real time — up to 1.5x more than AirPods 4 — so voices stay crisp in a busy café or on a noisy commute.

02

Adaptive Audio

Sound that adjusts to your environment in real time.

Adaptive Audio blends ANC and Transparency seamlessly, adjusting the level of noise you hear based on what's around you — no fiddling with controls.

03

Conversation Awareness

Automatically lowers media and focuses voices when you speak.

Start talking and your AirPods lower the media and focus on the voices in front of you — so you can chat without taking them out.

04

Wireless charging

Charge with USB-C, an Apple Watch charger, or a Qi-certified charger.

The Wireless Charging Case works with USB-C, an Apple Watch charger, or any Qi-certified charger — your case tops up wherever you happen to be.
